**FIRST Steamworks · 2017**  
**Team project:** Around 20 students, teachers and mentors

## Six weeks from concept to competition

Our FIRST Robotics team built a robot called **Woody** for the 2017 Steamworks season. The fixed deadline made mechanical reliability and rapid iteration as important as the initial design.

## My contributions

I helped prototype and test a ball-launching mechanism intended to reach approximately eight-foot-high goals, developed a winching concept for the game's rope-climbing task, and contributed to the robot's circuitry and software integration.

The robot made extensive use of wood to keep fabrication accessible and cost-conscious. The original portfolio records a **Creativity Award sponsored by Xerox** at an Ontario District Event.

## Why it still matters

This was an early introduction to designing mechanisms, writing control software, testing on actual hardware and making trade-offs as a multidisciplinary robotics team.
