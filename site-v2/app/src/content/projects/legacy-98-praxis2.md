**University of Toronto Engineering Science · Praxis II · 2018**

## Designing around a real user's constraints

I worked on a prototype intended to improve how residents of a Toronto long-term-care facility communicate with staff. The problem was not just electronic control; the interface had to be understandable and usable within a care setting.

## Prototype and implementation

The original project documents an **Arduino-based circuit**, speaker-control logic and changing colored-button interactions. My work included embedded code and circuit integration for the proposed communication interface.

This was a university engineering-design project. The original portfolio does not establish a long-term production deployment or measured clinical impact, so the description is limited to the prototype and intended use.

## What I learned

The project highlighted the need to design around operators and users, not just a technical subsystem. That principle later informed my approach to human-facing telemetry and flight-deck interfaces.
