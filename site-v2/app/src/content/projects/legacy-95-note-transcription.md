**Personal machine-learning project · 2020**  
**Tools:** Python, TensorFlow, OpenCV

## Turning sound into images

I explored whether a visual classifier could identify musical notes from spectrogram representations rather than analyzing raw waveforms directly. The project converted audio into spectrogram images, prepared a labeled dataset and trained a convolutional neural network to recognize note classes.

## Model development

The workflow included data preparation, training and evaluation, with experiments in augmentation, regularization and hyperparameter selection. The original portfolio reported **90% accuracy on its test dataset**; that figure is specific to the project's dataset and is not a claim of general transcription accuracy.
