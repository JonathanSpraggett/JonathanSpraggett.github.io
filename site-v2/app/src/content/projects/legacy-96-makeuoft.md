**MakeUofT · 2020 · Best Unconnected Hack (as recorded in the original portfolio)**

## A small autonomous system for a daily problem

Our team built a **Smart Flower Pot** prototype to explore how a mobile or sensor-assisted plant-care device could identify better light and moisture conditions. The idea combined environmental sensors and computer vision to monitor the plant's surroundings.

## Prototype components

The original project describes a system intended to measure soil moisture, observe available sunlight, detect the edge of its platform and assist with watering or repositioning decisions. It was a hackathon prototype, not a marketed or production-ready device.

## Demonstration and documentation

[View the original Devpost project](https://devpost.com/software/ipot-lite) · [Watch the hackathon presentation](https://youtu.be/R2XwOM9pS_0).

This project was an early opportunity to combine sensing, embedded hardware and autonomous decision-making outside a controlled classroom environment.
